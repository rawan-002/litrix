Scrape New Papers

For every researcher: checks the year of their latest stored paper, then fetches Scholar newest-first and stops as soon as an older paper appears — so only genuinely new papers are added. Existing papers are never re-downloaded or modified. Afterwards, every new paper goes through the Al-Baha affiliation verifier (OpenAlex → Crossref → PDF) — papers confirmed NOT Al-Baha are flagged and excluded from the dashboards.
