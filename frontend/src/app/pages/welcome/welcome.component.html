About Us

Built for the way researchers actually work.

Litrix is a cutting-edge platform designed to automate and streamline research data management. Our system helps researchers collect, organize, and analyze their academic publications and citations with unprecedented precision and intelligence.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don’t.

Automated Data Collection

Our platform automatically collects research data from trusted academic sources, keeping every publication record complete and effortlessly up-to-date.

Citation Analysis

Advanced analytics turn raw citation data into clear insights, showing how your research impact grows and evolves over time.

Duplicate Detection

Smart algorithms recognize and merge duplicate entries, keeping every researcher’s profile clean, accurate, and trustworthy.
